UNTO / FLOW

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

UNTO / FLOW is a daily index card and tomato focus timer. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and the choices you have. In short: your cards are yours. We don't sell your data, and the app has no ads and no trackers.

What we store

  • Without an account: everything you write (tasks, reflections, photos, timers and settings) stays on your device, in your browser's storage. Nothing is sent to us.
  • With an account: your email address and your password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, never in plain text), and a copy of your cards and settings so they sync between your devices.
  • Sign in with Google: we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and use them only to create and identify your account.

How we use it

  • To sync your cards across your devices and keep you signed in.
  • To send account emails you ask for, like password reset links.
  • To reply if you contact us.

We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell or share it with data brokers, and we don't read your cards.

Who helps us run the app

  • Supabase hosts accounts and synced cards (servers in the United States). Your data is protected so each account can only read its own cards.
  • Resend delivers account emails (such as password resets) from flow@untoworld.com.
  • Google handles "Sign in with Google" if you choose it.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's website. Like any web host, it may keep basic server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account. Your data then never leaves your device.
  • Sign out at any time from Account in the profile menu.
  • To delete your account and all synced data, email flow@untoworld.com from the email on your account. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm.
  • Ask us for a copy of your synced data at the same address.

Children

UNTO / FLOW isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. For important changes, we'll tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions? Email flow@untoworld.com.

隐私政策

UNTO / FLOW 是一款每日卡片和番茄专注计时器。你的内容属于你：我们不出售数据，没有广告，也没有追踪。

我们保存什么

  • 不登录：所有内容（待办、日记、照片、计时器和设置）都只保存在你的设备上，不会发给我们。
  • 登录后：保存你的邮箱、加密保存的密码，以及一份卡片和设置的副本，用于在设备之间同步。
  • 用 Google 登录：我们只获得你的姓名、邮箱和头像，只用于创建和识别你的账号。

服务提供方

账号和同步数据由 Supabase 托管（美国服务器），账号邮件由 Resend 发送，Google 登录由 Google 提供，网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。

你的选择

可以不登录使用；随时可以退出登录；如需删除账号及所有同步数据，请用账号邮箱发信至 flow@untoworld.com，我们会在 30 天内删除并回复确认。
